Plateau ratios span 2.3e-04 to 1.1e-02 -- a 49x spread across tensors, none near the quoted 1e-03.
But RMS(update)/lr averages 0.208 and spans only 0.195-0.248 -- flat across every tensor.
So the ratio factorises: RMS(dw)/RMS(w) = (0.21 * lr) / RMS(w). The optimizer sets the
numerator (an Adam step is bounded by lr; gradient noise damps it to ~0.21 lr here) and
the entire 49x spread is the denominator -- i.e. initialization scale. nn.Embedding inits
N(0,1) (RMS ~0.98); nn.Linear inits U(+/-1/sqrt(fan_in)) (RMS ~0.028).
Reading this as 'the embeddings are barely learning' would be wrong -- they take the same
size step as everything else. This is the asymmetry muP fixes by scaling LRs per tensor.
